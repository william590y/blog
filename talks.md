**[AI Safety and Existential Risk]({{ site.baseurl }}/aisafety/)**: a talk on how fast AI is advancing and why making it safe is urgent. It covers the trillion-dollar compute build-out, AI agents doing real engineering and research, rogue agents in the wild, the theory behind existential risk, and what we can do about it.
